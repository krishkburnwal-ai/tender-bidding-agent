3. MINIMUM ELIGIBILITY & QUALIFICATION CRITERIA (PRE-QUALIFICATION):
3.1 Financial Eligibility Criteria:
- The Bidder must have an Average Annual Turnover of at least INR 25.00 Crores (Twenty Five Crores) across the last three audited financial years (FY 2021-22, FY 2022-23, and FY 2023-24). Audited balance sheets with UDIN are mandatory.
- The Bidder must have a Positive Net Worth in each of the last three audited financial years.
- The Bidder must submit a Bank Solvency Certificate of minimum INR 6.00 Crores issued by a Scheduled Commercial Bank within 3 months of tender publication.
- Public Procurement Policy Relaxation: 50% relaxation in Turnover is permissible for registered MSEs and Startups as per Department of Expenditure guidelines.

3.2 Technical & Prior Experience Criteria:
- The Bidder must be an incorporated legal entity in India for at least five (5) completed years as of 31-March-2026.
- The Bidder must have successfully executed similar IT modernization / Cloud migration / e-Governance portal assignments in Central/State Government, PSUs, or Municipal Corporations meeting either of the following conditions during the last 5 years:
  a) One single completed contract of similar nature valued at not less than INR 12.00 Crores; OR
  b) Two completed contracts of similar nature valued at not less than INR 8.00 Crores each; OR
  c) Three completed contracts of similar nature valued at not less than INR 5.00 Crores each.
- Satisfactory Work Completion Certificates signed by an authorized client officer must be enclosed.

3.3 Quality & Security Certifications:
- The Bidder must possess valid certifications as on the bid submission date:
  a) ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management System)
  b) ISO 27001:2022 (Information Security Management System)
  c) CMMI Level 3 (or above) for Services or Development
  d) Empanelled partnership with MeitY-approved Cloud Service Providers (CSPs).

3.4 Statutory & General Declarations:
- Valid GSTIN registration and PAN Card.
- EPF and ESIC registration with latest deposit challans.
- Self-declaration on INR 100 Non-Judicial Stamp paper stating that the Bidder has not been debarred / blacklisted by any Government department or multilateral agency.

4. SCOPE OF WORK & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:
4.1 Cloud Infrastructure & Architecture:
- Architectural assessment, containerization (Docker / Kubernetes), and migration of existing portals to MeitY-empanelled Cloud Service Provider.
- Multi-Availability Zone deployment with active-active database clustering (PostgreSQL Enterprise / MySQL) ensuring RPO < 15 minutes and RTO < 1 hour.
- Implementation of Web Application Firewall (WAF), DDoS protection, and automated TLS 1.3 encryption.

4.2 AI Multilingual Citizen Services & Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI):
- Microservices-based modular redesign for high concurrency (> 50,000 concurrent citizen sessions).
- Integration of multilingual AI chatbots and automated NLP grievance classification in 12 Indian languages.
- Deep integration with National Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI):
  - Aadhaar e-KYC and Authentication via UIDAI APIs
  - DigiLocker document verification and certificate issuance
  - Payment Gateways (Bharat BillPay, UPI, Netbanking) with automated reconciliation.

4.3 24x7 Operations, Maintenance & SLA Commitments:
- Continuous 24x7 Tier-3 monitoring, security incident response, and performance tuning for 36 months.
- System Availability SLA: 99.95% monthly uptime.
- Incident Resolution SLA: Severity 1 (Critical) resolution within 2 hours; Severity 2 within 6 hours.
- Mandatory clearance of annual STQC and CERT-In empanelled cybersecurity audits prior to production deployments.

5. EVALUATION METHODOLOGY:
- Bids will be evaluated under Quality and Cost Based Selection (QCBS) with 70% weightage assigned to Technical Evaluation and 30% weightage to Financial Evaluation.
- Minimum qualifying score in Technical Bid Evaluation is 75 out of 100 marks. Only bidders achieving 75+ marks will be considered for financial bid opening.
